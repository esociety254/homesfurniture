OUR STORY

Crafted by
Kenyan hands

Founded in Nairobi in 2018, Malaba Homes was born from a simple belief: every Kenyan home deserves furniture that is both beautiful and built to last. We work with master craftsmen who have spent decades perfecting their art.

Every joint is hand-fitted. Every surface is finished by hand. We source only sustainably harvested African hardwood — Acacia, Mahogany, and Teak — from certified Kenyan forests.
